01 The principle

Every stage you add is a decision the music didn't ask for.

Most amplifiers are born with an error and spend the rest of the circuit correcting it. Negative feedback compares the output to the input, then feeds the difference back. It lowers distortion on paper. It also bends time: the correction arrives after the note.

SATRI starts somewhere else. Gain is set by the ratio of two resistors. The output device is asked to be accurate at the first attempt, so it never has to apologise for the first attempt. The signal travels forward; the timing stays intact.

The leading edge, the breath before the bow, the room after the last chord. All of it arrives when it should.

01

Gain by ratio

Two resistors set the amplification. No global feedback loop is needed to correct the stage, so there is no loop delay to smear the leading edge.

02

Current is a language

SATRI-LINK carries the signal as current over BNC. Preamplifier and power amplifier speak directly, with no voltage conversion in between.

03

Fast enough to disappear

The SATRI circuit's delay is about one ten-millionth of a second. The CAP-1001M reaches 600 kHz and stays flat to 100 kHz.

04

Both inputs, one circuit

HBK(I), now in its second generation, accepts voltage or current at the input without a buffer stage. Your source chooses the language.

04 The craft

Still a workshop.
On purpose.

Bakoon Products was founded in Kumamoto in 1974. Fifty years later, the company is still deliberately small: one bench, one small team, and a designer who works in the same room where the amplifiers are assembled, measured, and heard.

We stay small because it is the only way to hear the last five percent. Small is what we protect. A factory optimises for the average unit. A workshop can hear the difference between this one and the last one.

05 The record

Heritage, and
state of the art.

The SATRI circuit has been revised, measured, and rebuilt for thirty-seven years. Every generation removed something: a capacitor, a conversion, a correction. The latest HBK(I) is in its second generation, in current production. This is the record.

  1. 1974

    Bakoon Products is founded

    Audio manufacturing begins in Kumamoto, Japan.

  2. 1989

    SATRI V1.0

    The first SATRI amplifier, MORPHEUS. Gain set by two resistors; no global feedback.

  3. 1998

    The first SATRI-IC

    BPM-7110T hybrid IC. Super-SATRI output stages follow in the AMP-5520 series.

  4. 2007

    SATRI V9.0

    A new ultra-precision current mirror cuts distortion to one tenth. SATRI-IC-SP enters production.

  5. 2011

    SATRI V9.3

    Base-current error is corrected. SATRI-IC-EX and the higher-precision UL follow.

  6. 2013

    Best Sound, HIGH END Munich

    The circuit is recognised on the international stage.

  7. 2014

    AMP-5521

    A fully surface-mount amplifier, and a new generation of circuits built around it.

  8. 2016

    AMP-KUMAMOTO

    A limited model made in the wake of the Kumamoto earthquakes.

  9. 2019

    MJ Technology of the Year

    CAP-1001 and CAP-1004 both take awards from MJ Wireless & Experiment.

  10. 2026

    HBK(I), second generation

    CAP-1001M introduces the latest input topology. SATRI V12 appears in the CAP-1005 DAC.
